# Le Lovophon - teaser and prototype requirements

Updated 11 November 2026. R5 is the current nominal CAD; this document states requirements and options, not a fabrication or performance release.

## Confirmed requirements

- Twelve strings of the same nominal gauge and material, with individual tension adjustment and movable third bridges establishing the speaking lengths.
- Preserve the chosen exact pentatonic pitch/drive ratios, proportional arm lengths and ideal eight-minute repeat at regulated 3 rpm input.
- Four curved rear-only support ribs on one rail preserve the open string bed and visible pendulums. This R4/R5 direction supersedes the four-corner R3 frame. Intermediate shaft and station bearings remain explicitly supported.
- Make the gear-to-pendulum connecting links visually slimmer while retaining adequate stiffness, serviceable eyes/joints and verified motion clearances.
- Keep the instrument light, sturdy, serviceable and portable in one hand. Prefer standard hardware and Allen-key adjustment.

## Teaser concept: protokoto for the later build

The concept piece being developed for the teaser will use a protokoto that meets the design requirements of the intended later physical build. It is the later-build design target, rather than a purely decorative stand-in.

- Carry the same twelve equal-gauge strings, proportional speaking lengths, tension adjustment, movable bridges, exact pitch/drive ratios, proportional arms and eight-minute cycle into the teaser design.
- Show the current four rear curved ribs, slender connecting links, accessible serviceable parts, and credible anchor, bridge, bearing and structural load paths. Check revised geometry and motion clearances before treating it as a working arrangement.
- Keep future manufacture of the wooden body by a wood shop feasible through an explicit part breakdown and accessible materials/processes. The dimensioned cutting sheet remains a later deliverable.
- Preserve the intended koto-style body in the concept work. The possible solid-body first test prototype is a separate development route and does not replace the later-build target shown in the teaser.
- Keep the teaser design and later engineering definition consistent as dimensions and materials are settled. The existing R2 CAD/film is not automatically the revised protokoto, and rendered appearance or sampled audio does not establish physical performance.

## First-prototype body option

The first prototype MAY use a solid body/base. This is a permitted prototype route, not a final commitment to solid-body construction.

Its immediate purpose is to establish real string tension, tuning stability, repeatable pick release, drivetrain operation and measured loads. Development of a hollow acoustic soundbox can follow. A hollow body is not required before the first mechanical prototype can proceed.

Anchor, tensioner, bridge and mechanism interfaces should be documented so a later soundbox can reuse the proven modules where practical. Body stiffness and the full twelve-string load still need checking with a solid base.

## Future wood-shop deliverable

Keep the ability to commission the wooden body parts from a wood shop as a design requirement. Do NOT prepare or issue a cutting sheet now.

When the body design is sufficiently settled, provide:

- Dimensioned assembly and individual-part drawings in millimetres.
- A cutting list with part identifiers, quantities and finished dimensions.
- Material, grain direction, thickness and finish requirements.
- Joint, rebate, groove, drilling and fastener details, including which operations are left for final fitting.
- Anchor/tuner and rear-frame mounting interfaces.
- Clear revision/status marking and a distinction between acoustic trial blanks and settled manufacturing dimensions.

The construction should use accessible stock materials and ordinary shop processes; CNC routing or outsourced machining may supply repeatability where useful.

## Dimensions and string choices still provisional

The 600 mm bass speaking length, approximately 650-700 mm overall length, 1.05 mm fluorocarbon candidate, approximately 38 N per string, and 2 mm steel connecting-rod centres are study values. None is a frozen cutting dimension or a verified working rating. Same-gauge nylon fishing monofilament remains an alternative.


## Child-powered drivetrain goal

A school-age child should be able to start and continuously hand-crank the complete instrument comfortably at its nominal operating speed. Size the drivetrain for combined plucking load, starting/running gear and bearing friction, linkage resistance and alignment sensitivity.

This is a requirement for force, load and friction. Include the real gear meshes, bearings, dry link bushes, thrust stacks and manufactured alignment in the effort budget. Measure breakaway torque, running torque and maximum coincidence load through the complete eight-minute cycle. No numerical hand-force acceptance threshold has been agreed yet; record measured values and uncertainty before claiming the goal has been achieved. The ideal string-force calculation alone does not establish it.
