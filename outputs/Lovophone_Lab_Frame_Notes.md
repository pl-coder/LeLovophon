# Le Lovophone — open laboratory frame

A compact, exposed-structure revision of the twelve-string pentatonic Lovophone. The string bank and soundbox share the same transverse centre. The rear drive occupies an open outrigger bay, at approximately the previous overall size (394 × 203 × 197 mm).

## What changed

- Soundbox width reduced from 200 to 140 mm, centred at the existing string-bank centre. Its length and height remain unchanged. The open rear drive frame retains the compact overall scale; the nominal CAD envelope differs by less than 1 mm in width.
- Four slim posts flank the string bank, with triangular end stays, rear stays and base ties. The end frames sit beyond the speaking strings and saddles.
- Existing shared upper rails, musical pivots and removable bearing hangers retained. Telescoping end extensions connect the rails to the new end frames.
- Large low drive plates replaced by open triangular bearing brackets, retaining the bearing, axle and bolt locations.
- Rear rubber feet now connect to the open base rails through metal stems.
- Visible brass-coloured clamp blocks and ordinary mounting tabs express the proposed removable connections. The motor and controls are retained in CAD but hidden in the saved presentation and renders.

## Music and motion

String positions, gauges, speaking lengths, third bridges, arm lengths, eccentric cams, follower geometry and rhythmic ratios are unchanged. Existing pentatonic MIDI remains valid at 120 BPM, with video time zero aligned to MIDI time zero. Narrowing the soundbox will change its acoustic response; that requires physical listening tests.

The editable Blender scene includes the eight-minute, normal-speed animation, with frame 1 at MIDI zero, 24 frames per second. No new full-length video is included in this revision.

## Materials and construction intent

The provisional frame uses 8 × 1, 6 × 1 and 4 × 1 mm aluminium tubing, plus 2.5 mm steel base ties. These are concept sizes, not a verified structural specification. Tube cut lengths in the parts table describe nominal geometry and need allowances for the chosen real joints. Availability should be checked locally before fixing stock sizes.

Clamps are proposed as split, bolted fittings. Their split lines, fastener details, axial retention and preload are not fully detailed. Several structural members meet/intersect at intended joints; their fishmouths, clevises, collars, mounting reliefs and disassembly sequence need production detailing. Crossed base ties need separation or a proper centre junction. The open drive brackets are cut-and-drilled 4 mm stock concepts.

The model does not establish minimum weight, strength, joint rigidity, fatigue life or safe child operation. It is a visual and kinematic prototype, not a fabrication-ready machine. Use removable local guards where a physical prototype requires them.
