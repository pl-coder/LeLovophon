# L’Harpe d’Amour — open centred portable study

The complete six-module bank is centred on the soundbox, retaining its 24 mm module spacing. Pick positions run from X=95 to 215 mm, centred at X=155 mm. The transparent enclosure has been removed.

The assembled model occupies 370 × 200 × 179 mm (length × depth × height). A provisional semi-hard case needs approximately 400 × 230 × 210 mm of clear internal space, with its padding supporting the soundbox and leaving the mechanism clear. This is a packing allowance, not a selected or tested case. The current design fits this envelope assembled; routine transport should not require releasing string tension or dismantling the bank.

Modelled material mass is approximately 2.19 kg, using assumed densities of plywood 650, aluminium 2700, steel 7850, polymer 1200 and rubber 1100 kg/m³. This is an estimate from part volumes, not a weighed prototype. It excludes the unmodelled clockwork, fasteners, finishes and case. A useful design budget is 3 kg for the completed instrument and 4 kg including its handled case; these are targets pending component selection.

The soundbox is 45 mm deep with a 3 mm removable lid, 6 mm sides, 4 mm bottom, end reinforcement and two lightened internal ribs. The upper bearing plates are 4 mm aluminium with closed bearing bores; the outboard plates have diagonal braces. Each module has a locating shoe with two M4 clearance holes, with matching holes through the removable soundboard. Screw engagement, captive fasteners, bearing retention and the complete mounting load path still need detailing before manufacture. The open layout provides cleaning and adjustment access.

The six equal full-span strings retain separate third bridges for speaking lengths 330, 293⅓, 264, 247½, 220 and 198 mm. The C–D–E–F–G–A timing ratios remain 1, 9/8, 5/4, 4/3, 3/2 and 5/3. The wider swings remain approximately 30–39 degrees total. The current 4:1 swing amplification is represented by 64:16 timing-pulley pitch envelopes.

All six picks intersect their corresponding strings geometrically when pointing straight down. A 72-position-per-cycle check for each voice found no unintended moving-to-fixed solid intersections, and all part shapes passed the native validity check. These are sampled geometric checks, not proof of a functioning pluck, adequate clearance tolerance or a physically viable drive. Moving-to-moving contact, compliant pick release, string tension, cam contact force, spring sizing, bearing loads, belt tooth profiles, belt tensioning, fatigue and acoustics require further engineering and prototype testing. The clockwork power source is represented by its input interface only.
