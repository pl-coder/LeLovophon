# R4 frame assembly and service

The drawing uses X along the bank, Y vertically upwards, and Z across the strings. The bass end of the body is CAD X=-350 mm; the bottom of the wooden body is Y=-172 mm. All mounting positions in the CSV also have simple bass-end/bottom datums. Rib start positions are 101, 294, 468 and 671 mm from the bass end. Three feet project right along the rear rim; the last one projects left/inboard to clear the tuners.

1. Have the four identical ribs, crank outrigger and backing plates cut and deburred. Prepare the four 48 mm stock angles to their supplied 3D hole patterns; check the inside root radius against the rib before assembly.
2. With the removable bottom off, prepare the structural rear-rim holes and open-bottom nut/tool pockets shown in R4 CAD. Insert the sixteen metal compression sleeves, fitted to the actual rim thickness. Mount the four angles with M4 through-bolts, outside/inside washers and internal backing plates. Keep the thin soundboard out of this bolt-preload stack.
3. Attach each 8 mm rib to its angle with two M5 bolts, head-side washers and nuts. Leave the joints adjustable initially. Fit four SHF8 clamps to the rib pads with two M5 bolts each.
4. Slide the 629 mm long, 8 mm solid fixed rail through the four clamps and the retained mechanism carriers. Do not force a bowed rail through tight clamps. Set it straight in an assembly jig, align the existing 5 mm input shaft and bearings, then tighten progressively. Follow the selected component suppliers' fastener/clamp instructions; final torques and locking method must be established on the prototype. No final bolt-preload/wood-strength approval is implied by the CAD.
5. Fit the fifth SHF8 clamp in reverse orientation to the 6 mm crank outrigger. Bolt the outrigger to the existing R3 crank bearing console using its two 4 mm spacers, washers on both sides and M4 × 25 bolts. The end flange bolts are reversed relative to the stand bolts to maintain clearance. Keep the original crank bearing, retainer and freely rotating grip stack.
6. Turn the hand crank slowly with the picks initially clear and the strings untensioned. Check every bearing, gear, link and rod for free movement throughout the cycle; verify the clamps are fixed while the 5 mm input shaft rotates freely. Establish alignment and loaded frame stiffness before setting pick depth or string tension. Set gear phase using the original R3 records if anything has been dismantled.
7. Bring the string load up in stages and test one voice first. Check frame movement, clamp slip, nut retention, rail straightness and actual pick release under operating load. The current nominal checks do not cover these physical effects. Record the accepted settings before extending to all twelve voices.

For routine cleaning, remove the bottom for rear nut access. Use hex keys for screw heads/clamp adjustments and 7 mm and 8 mm spanners to hold M4/M5 nuts during assembly. Do not withdraw the structural rail or dismantle a bearing stack under string load. R3 is preserved separately; this file is the new R4 candidate.

The instrument's tuning, proportional arms, 3 rpm input reference and 8 minute repeat are unchanged. Turning the crank by hand gives the same rational sequence of 24 input turns; the elapsed 8 minutes depends on holding 3 rpm. The frame is not a speed regulator.
