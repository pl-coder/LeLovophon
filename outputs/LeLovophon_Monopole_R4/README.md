# Le Lovophon — curved rear stand R4

R2's rear-only support layout, rebuilt as four identical curved 8 mm aluminium ribs. The front and string bed are open. The ribs sweep behind the moving parts and carry a fixed 8 mm steel rail; a cut 6 mm outrigger supports the retained hand crank. The feet use stock angle, through-bolts, metal compression sleeves and internal backing plates. The new frame needs no welding.

R3's 700×220×60 mm body, twelve strings, bridge positions, proportional arms, pitch/rotation ratios, thin links and crank mechanism remain. Every moving solid was compared to R3 and retained without geometric change. At 3 rpm the repeat remains 480 seconds; hand-cranking preserves the turn relationships, while elapsed time follows hand speed.
